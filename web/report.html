TimeWhatDetail

Contactless 60 GHz radar, nothing worn. Sleep stages come from the radar's built-in estimate. A breathing dip is a sharp slow-down in breathing while you were still. A pause in breathing looks like a dip, but so do normal changes during sleep, so dips are not an apnea count. In testing, Stillwave caught 8 of 8 deliberate breath holds and logged about 5 dips an hour in a normal night. This is not a diagnosis; sleep clinics use airflow and oxygen sensors.
